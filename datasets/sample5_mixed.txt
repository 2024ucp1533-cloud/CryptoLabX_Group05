In 2024, the CryptoLabX team began developing a modular framework for teaching
classical and modern cryptography. The project is divided into several stages:
Stage 1 covers project setup and file handling; Stage 2 introduces classical
ciphers such as Caesar, Vigenere, and Playfair; Stage 3 focuses on cryptanalysis
techniques including frequency analysis and brute-force attacks; and Stage 4
explores modern cryptographic primitives like AES and RSA. Team members are
expected to document their progress, write clean code, and use Git for version
control throughout the semester. Good luck, and happy hacking!
